About Us

Shaping Tomorrow’s Leaders Through Innovation and Excellence

Nalanda Vidya Niketan has been a trusted name in education since 1988. Our +1 & +2 programs are designed to empower students with essential 21st-century skills such as critical thinking, problem-solving, and adaptability. Through structured IIT/NEET preparation and skill-building activities, we ensure students excel academically while nurturing their leadership potential.

Our focus on holistic development combines academics with extracurricular activities, personality development, and career guidance. At Nalanda, we shape individuals who are prepared to lead in a dynamic world.
